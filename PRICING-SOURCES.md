Beauty, lifestyle, product, newborn and celebrity services use a tailored quote rather than an unverified fixed price. Makeup has an advice page with a separate quote request, not a claim that makeup is included in photography packages.

Where the source mixes unrelated group sizes or evening inclusions into a solo/hotel package, those inconsistent claims are not imported. The duration, price, photo count and applicable location count are retained; participant counts are shown where the service-specific source is clear.
